La Reina del Draft 👑“I didn’t ask for a crown. I drafted one.” 🌶️
—
👋 New to fantasy football? This page tells you who to draft and why, in plain English. Each turn: read the top card, glance at its colored badge, then click “Draft to my team” on your pick — or “Someone else took him” when another manager picks a player. The board and advice update automatically.
What do all these football words mean? (tap to learn the game)

In the list below, the green me button = you drafted him; grey off = another team did. Press Enter for “someone took him”, Shift+Enter for “draft to me”.

⭐ Who should I pick?

🏈 My team

    🔮 Draft Planner

    📅 Season Manager Running your real Sleeper team on live weekly data. It sets your best lineup for this week, projects your head-to-head matchup, finds waiver upgrades, and grades trades.

    🥊 This Week’s Matchup

    Your projected head-to-head. Win odds come from both lineups’ projected points and how swingy a fantasy week is.

    ⚔️ Position Battle

    You vs. your opponent, slot by slot. Win odds = your chance of outscoring them at that spot this week. Green = you’re favored, red = you’re behind.

    🏟️ Games & Odds

    Every NFL game your players (and your opponent’s) are in — kickoff, place, and the Vegas line. Total = expected combined points (higher = more scoring); Implied = points that team is expected to score (higher is better for your guys).

    ✅ Start These Players

    Your highest-scoring legal lineup for this week — this is who you should start. Players on bye or injured are handled automatically.

    ➕ Waiver-Wire Targets

    Free agents (players nobody rostered) who’d raise your lineup. +pts = points gained after dropping your listed player; 🔥 = how many managers are adding him right now (grab before your league does).

    🔁 Trade Analyzer

    Type player names you'd give and get (comma-separated). It grades the trade by the swing in your starting-lineup points — positive means you come out ahead.

    🏈 Your Roster

    🏆 Conquest Board Your season isn’t a bracket — you play everyone in a round-robin (Weeks 1–14), then the top 6 records make the playoff bracket (Weeks 15–17). Here’s who’s winning, who you’ve smashed, and who’s left.

    📊 Standings

    Ranked by record, then points scored. The dashed line is the playoff cutoff — top 6 get in. Before games start, this shows projected order by roster strength.

    🗡️ Your Conquest

    Every team on your schedule. WON = smashed, LOST = revenge pending, NEXT/UP = still to come.

    🥊 Playoff Bracket Weeks 15–17

    The single-elimination bracket. Seeds #1–2 get a first-round bye. Before Week 15 it shows the projected seeds by current standing.

    📅 Results by Week

    The whole league at a glance — each team’s W/L path. green = win, red = loss, — = still to play. Your row is highlighted.
